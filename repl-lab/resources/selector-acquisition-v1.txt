This is selector acquisition, not the older workflow acquisition experiment.
Propose an analysis artifact with interface {:name "select-route" :arity 1}, no
dependencies and parent nil. Its function receives {:observation ... :skills ...}.
The observation contains only current scheduling information. Skills have reviewed
interfaces and limitations. Full approved source is available through artifact/inspect.

The selector returns one of these exact shapes:
{:route :reuse :hash "<approved policy hash>"}
{:route :compose :hashes ["<approved rank hash>" "<approved packer hash>"]}
{:route :compute}
{:route :adapt}
{:route :synthesize}
{:route :delegate}

Reuse executes the selected approved policy. Compose connects the approved ranker
to the approved general packer. Compute executes the general resource scheduler
without inference. Adapt and synthesize open fresh model sessions to produce a
task-local policy; adapt supplies approved interfaces and source on request.
Delegate opens a real child model session to propose a task-local policy. Every
root, child, inspection and generation call consumes the same declared budgets.
These are capabilities, not recommendations about when to choose each route.

You may query learning and development observations, inspect sources, run SCI,
call child sessions and stage candidates. development/evaluate tests a staged
selector on development cases only. The controller applies a fixed independent
acceptance rule. Your final summary cannot approve a proposal. Hidden outcomes, future faults, actual durations and evaluation seeds are unavailable.
Finish with :done after staging the selector. Do not propose a workflow artifact.
