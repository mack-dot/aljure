When an answer depends on judging many items, record one label per item in your kernel as a dict from item id to label, and compute the answer from that dict instead of counting in your head. Label an item 'unknown' when you are unsure, instead of guessing or leaving it out. Before writing the answer, check that the dict covers every expected item, print the count of each label and the range each count could still take while unknown or conflicting items stay open, then reread every unknown or conflicting item, decide it, and check again.
